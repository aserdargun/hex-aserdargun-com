# Visual concept brief

Tool: built-in `imagegen`, UI mockup generation. Output: `HEX/previews/design-concept.png`.

Prompt direction: A complete, airy desktop engineering explorer with pale gray #f4f5f2 canvas, charcoal text and vermilion #d94d2e accent. A 72px masthead with HEX, Explorer, Learning path, ENG and EN/TR. A narrow numbered rail for Explore, Structure, Joints, Actuation, Sensors, Power, Compute, Perception, Control and Behavior. A large actual 3D humanoid viewport with front/back/side camera controls, named camera/knee callouts and an assembled-to-exploded slider. A 300px lesson rail with “More than a machine.” and Sense → Estimate → Control → Actuate → Sense again. CTA “Explore the knee”. Footer connects Mechanics → Intelligence.

Primary title: “A body. A connected system.” Supporting copy: “Explore the engineering behind embodied intelligence.” The humanoid is a neutral research prototype with exposed machined metal links, modest neutral polymer panels, graphite/copper actuator housings, two head cameras, a low battery and grounded feet. No fictional armor, neon circuitry or decorative metrics. UI controls remain code-native; the final robot is authored in Blender and rendered interactively from its GLB.

The complete tool prompt is preserved in the task's image-generation call. The generated concept is a compositional reference; the authored geometry follows the independently reasoned mechanical architecture and the exact same model is used in the web experience and Blender deliverables.
